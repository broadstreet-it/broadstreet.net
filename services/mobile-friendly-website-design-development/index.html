Mobile Website Design Services

Looking at a website from a mobile device is a very different experience than using a desktop computer. There are a number of mobile design features that are important to consider when designing a website for mobile devices, for example:

  • Load times - Mobile users expect fast-loading pages. If a mobile site takes too long to load, visitors are likely to abandon it and seek alternatives. A slow site frustrates users and negatively impacts their experience. The longer a webpage takes to load, the higher its bounce rate. Bounce rate refers to the percentage of users who leave a site after viewing only one page. High bounce rates signal to search engines that users find the content unhelpful, leading to lower rankings. Search engines prioritize faster-loading sites.Slow mobile sites may be penalized by ranking lower in search results, especially for mobile users. Ecommerce sites, in particular, suffer if their checkout process is slow. Even a slight delat can lead to lost customers. Research shows that 53% of mobile users abandon a page if it takes longer than three seconds to load.

  • Menus - A typical menu on a mobile device uses what we call a "hamuger menu", which is 3 horizontal lines that pop up when you click on it. On a desktop or laptop computer, however, we typically have a large navigation menu that covers the whole screen. 

  • Clicking - There is no double-clicking on a mobile device, and also no mouse-over actions, so for example, to see a drop-down menu on a mobile device, you have to click it, where on a computer you may just be able to hover your mouse over the menu item.  

  • Navigation - Interaction on mobile devices relies heavily on touch gestures like tapping, swiping, and pinch-to-zoom. Buttons and links need to be finger-friendly and well-spaced.
