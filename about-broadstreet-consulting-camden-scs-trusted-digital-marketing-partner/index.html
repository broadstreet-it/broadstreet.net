Local roots. A wider perspective.

Meet Broadstreet, a Camden, SC digital marketing company helping businesses with websites, SEO, Google Ads, and ongoing support for over 20 years.

Broadstreet Consulting is a Camden, South Carolina-based digital marketing and web development agency helping small businesses in Camden, Lancaster, and across the Midlands grow their online presence and increase sales. As a Google Partner certified in Google Ads, our team combines local know-how with proven digital strategy to help businesses attract and engage customers through the web and social media. Broadstreet also specializes in Drupal, the open-source content management system that powers flexible, scalable business websites.

A Long History Serving South Carolina Businesses Online

The Broadstreet team has been working on the internet since before most local businesses had websites at all. We built our first website in 1994 and have been helping South Carolina businesses do business online since 1998. What started as a small operation in Wagener, SC has grown into a full-service digital marketing agency serving clients throughout Camden, Lancaster, and the surrounding Midlands region. Today, our focus is on building dynamic online communities and digital strategies that help local businesses compete and grow.

Why the Name "Broadstreet"

by Tom Sliker

Growing up on the outskirts of Trenton, New Jersey, in a small town called Yardville, I had the run of a very safe neighborhood — with one rule: never cross Broad Street. It was a busy, dangerous highway that required real experience and judgment to navigate. Some people got hurt crossing it. Some didn't make it across at all.

That memory stuck with me. When I thought about the thousands of small businesses eager to tap into the power of the internet but unsure how to get there, Broad Street came to mind again. So many local business owners can see the opportunity the web offers, but the path to get there feels unfamiliar and intimidating. That's exactly why Broadstreet Consulting exists: to help Camden, Lancaster, and Midlands-area business owners navigate the internet with confidence. We're here to offer a hand, share our experience, and help you cross over.

Broadstreet Consulting Is a Google Partner

Broadstreet Consulting is proud to hold Google Partner status — a distinction Google reserves for agencies it recognizes as trustworthy, high-performing, and current on best practices. As a certified Google Partner serving the Camden, SC area, we're listed on Google's official Partner Search, giving local business owners an easy way to verify our credentials. Partner status also gives our team access to exclusive Google training, product updates, and industry research, keeping us current as search and advertising platforms evolve.

To maintain this status, we must continuously meet Google's standards for certified staff, service quality, and account performance — a badge that can be, and is, revoked from agencies that stop meeting the bar. That means when you work with Broadstreet Consulting, you get a double layer of quality assurance: our own standards, and Google's.

Our Story: From Wagener, SC to the Camden Midlands

February 1992 — Sliker Office Solutions (SOS) is founded to provide bookkeeping and secretarial services to small businesses in Wagener, South Carolina.

May 1994 — SOS expands into Microsoft-based software development, building custom Access and Visual Basic applications.

October 1994 — Founder Tom Sliker builds his first website.

May 1995 — SOS helps launch one of the first hospital intranets for a large regional health care organization.

1996 — 2001 — Tom rides out the dot-com boom (and bust) of the late '90s, registering the sliker.com domain along the way.

September 2006 — SOS refocuses on affordable website and e-commerce solutions built specifically for small businesses.

December 2007 — Broadstreet Consulting is formed, assembling an international team of developers and consultants to deliver affordable web solutions, e-commerce, and online community platforms.

April 2008 — All client websites are migrated to Drupal.

January 2010 — Broadstreet rolls out ongoing support plans and adds email and social media marketing services for clients.
