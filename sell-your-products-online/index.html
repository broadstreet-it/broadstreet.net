Today, more than ever, it's so important for local businesses to expand their reach and stay relevant by selling online. As today's economy continues shifting more and more towards convenience and efficiency, the internet has dramatically changed the way people take in information, find local businesses and shop. Finding reviews, comparing businesses, prices and quality has never been easier for a consumer. Retailers need to have a strong internet and digital marketing strategy, including eCommerce, just to stay afloat. We have years of experience creating fully-integrated eCommerce websites, allowing you to sell your products online, and working with business owners or managers to develop and implement a digital marketing strategy to drive business through their website.

There are several different ways that you can sell your products online, and we work with our clients to help them on the platform of their choice. Drupal, Wordpress, Squarespace, Shopify, and Square - sound a little overwhelming? We can work with you to help you understand the differences and figure out what is best for your business and budget. We can help you get started from nothing, or we can help you improve what you already have. The benefits of working with Broadstreet Consulting to build your eCommerce & online marketing strategy are that you will be working with a local team who is hands-on. From our first meeting forward, our goal is to get to know you and your business and implement strategies that are custom fit to help your business grow. Our expert internet marketing team can help you with so much more than eCommerce support. Some other services we offer our local businesses are through reputation management, search engine optimization (SEO), graphic & web design, content creation & social media marketing. While Broadstreet works with many eCommerce platforms, our team has developed expertise in Drupal, which is our primary & recommended approach for building eCommerce solutions. Drupal is an open-source (free) content management system, when used along with Ubercart, an open-source shopping cart solution, we can develop a fully functional online store customized to your specific requests. A Drupal Commerce solution will allow for much more flexibility, the ability to easily add custom features rather than the out of the box solutions offered by other mediums. This type of site will take a little more effort to build and will be a little more costly to maintain, this is why we know it's not the right solution for every business. But if you can afford the best, we want to give it to you.

Our Drupal Commerce solution allows for flexibility:

  • quickly add and update products and pricing
  • maintain secure payments and credit card processing
  • integrate your site with back-end inventory and ordering systems
  • implement completely custom feature within your website

Not only will we design and build the site for you, but we also will then:

  • help you develop the processes you need for order processing, customer service, and fulfillment
  • teach your team how to update, add, and remove products and content on the eCommerce website
  • develop and implement digital marketing strategies to grow your sales
