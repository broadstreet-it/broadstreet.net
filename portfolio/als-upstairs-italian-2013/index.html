Al's Upstairs Italian

Explore Broadstreet’s work with Al's Upstairs Italian: website design, online presence, and a partnership built around the client’s business.

Al's Upstairs is one of the finest Italian restaurants in Columbia, SC.  While they had a basic Website in place for a number of years, they realized that today's marketplace is changing, and they needed a new design, better content, and a mobile-friendly website.   

Broadstreet implemented a new responsive design using the Drupal Omega base theme.  According to Tracy at Al's Upstairs, the improve search results on Google Search and Google Maps, along with a sleek mobile-friendly design, has greatly increased the number of travelers "passing through" who will now stop in Columbia to visit Al's Upstairs. 

We sent in our photographic team to get some nice photos of their delicious food, making for a mouth-watering slideshow on the front page. 

Along with these dramatic changes, Broadstreet helped to evaluate restaurant reservation systems and assisted with the selection of Urban Spoon.  

On an ongoing basis, Broadstreet helps Al's Upstairs with social media marketing and online reputation management.  Check out the many fine reviews at Urban Spoon and Trip Advisor.
