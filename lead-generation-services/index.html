Our Process

We help you develop your own internet marketing strategy, through as many consultations that we need, together we will design a strategy that will succeed.

We can start from scratch, take hold of existing accounts, or venture only into specific areas of online marketing together. 



1. CONSULTATION

Before starting any project, Broadstreet Consulting offers free consultations over the phone or in-person to discuss your business goals, what internet marketing services you need, functions & ideas that will set your company ahead of the competition. Once we determine your needs we will give you a free quote based on all the information we — ve gathered.



2. AGREEMENT AND DOWN PAYMENT

Once you've looked over your detailed quote, if you've decided to move forward with lead generation or any of our internet marketing services, we will draw up an agreement based on our previous consultations and quote including the estimated completion date. Once we receive a signed agreement & down payment, we will move to the next stage in our process.

3. PLANNING AND LAYOUT

Together, we will create a blueprint for your online marketing strategy based on our agreement. We will go over every detail together so that you can offer any changes or ideas before the design & development stage.



4. DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

During the design & programming stage, we will be busy making all of your visions a reality. From a static preview of the internet strategy we design together, our team will put together a live demo for you to view and test that represents your brand, focuses on a niche market, and is built to generate very targeted leads. The length of time will depend on the project, and will be projected to you in our agreement. 



5. LIVE DEMO

When our project is 80% complete, we will hold a webinar phone or meet in person to review a live demo together. We encourage as much involvement and collaboration as possible during this stage. We want to ensure that every feature and every function is to your satisfaction.



6. CHANGES

After both of our teams have viewed and tested your live demo, we will consider and work on changes that need to be made as quickly as possible. After the changes have been submitted, completed, and customer-approved, we will move on to the final stage in our process.



7. LAUNCH

When we launch your lead generation based internet marketing strategy, our staff will run a series of diagnostics to ensure that all the functions are intact and that every application runs seamlessly without any lags or broken links. If problems arise within this phase, they are fixed until we have met every standard.



HOSTING & SUPPORT

Once everything is working and functioning properly, we have a few different options that we will have discussed during the creation of our agreement. We offer our basic website hosting services, as well as ongoing monthly support, in which we provide regular help with your website & internet marketing strategies. Our most successful customers have joined our monthly support plans in which we provide ongoing SEO services, web developments & updates, content creation, Google AdWords, social media & email marketing services.
